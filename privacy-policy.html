OnlyFood

Privacy Policy

OnlyFood is designed to help you discover recipes, save favorites, and share meals with friends. This app is designed to work primarily with data stored on your own device.

Overview

OnlyFood does not require an account, and most app preferences are stored locally on your device. We may show ads in the app using Google AdMob. We do not sell your personal information.

What data the app stores

To make the app work properly, OnlyFood stores some preferences locally on your device, such as:

  • selected language
  • dietary preferences and cuisine selections
  • saved recipes
  • temporary daily likes
  • food type preferences

This information is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage and is not sent to us by the app.

Sharing recipes

If you choose to share a recipe, OnlyFood uses the standard iOS share sheet to let you send recipe content yourself. The app only prepares the content you choose to share and hands it off to iOS. What happens after that depends on the app or service you select for sharing.

Permissions

The current version of OnlyFood does not ask for access to sensitive device permissions such as your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library in order to function.

Tracking and analytics

We do not run our own behavioral analytics or build user profiles inside OnlyFood. If ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process limited technical information related to ad delivery, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and aggregated reporting.

Advertising

OnlyFood may display ads provided by Google AdMob. We intend to use simple, non-personalized ads rather than ads based on your activity across other apps or websites. Even for non-personalized ads, Google may use limited technical data such as IP address, coarse location, device and app information, advertising identifiers where available, diagnostics, and ad interaction data for purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention, and reporting.

Depending on your region, you may also be shown privacy or consent choices before ads are served. For more information about how Google processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's privacy

OnlyFood is a recipe and food discovery app and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes in the future. If we add features that collect or process personal data, this page will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact
